Christian Worldview

My Christian worldview begins with the belief that God is the Creator of all things and that life has meaning because it is made in His image.

God and Creation

I believe that God is holy, loving, and personal, and that the universe was created by His wisdom and power. This shapes the way I see both the world and myself. Nothing is accidental, and every person carries value because they are created by God.

Jesus Christ and Salvation

Central to my faith is the belief that Jesus Christ is the Son of God who came to save humanity. His life, death, and resurrection show God's love and offer forgiveness, hope, and new life to all who trust in Him.

The Bible as Guiding Truth

I see the Bible as the inspired Word of God and the foundation for understanding truth, morality, and purpose. It teaches me how to live with integrity, compassion, and humility in a world that often values selfishness over service.

How This Shapes Daily Life

My Christian worldview affects the way I treat others, make decisions, and respond to hardship. I try to live with gratitude, kindness, and faith, trusting that God is present even in difficult times.

Hope and Purpose

Above all, my faith gives me hope. I believe life is not only about the present, but also about a greater purpose and the promise of eternal life with God. That truth gives me direction, peace, and strength.
